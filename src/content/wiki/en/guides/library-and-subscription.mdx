The **35-song Decades of Hits release** and **Just Dance+** are different sources of content. [Ubisoft’s launch announcement](https://news.ubisoft.com/en-us/article/4C0I0ak0v6u63UZkEdVaEl/just-dance-decades-of-hits-out-now-with-mamma-mia-by-abba-wannabe-by-spice-girls-manchild-by-sabrina-carpenter-and-more) explains that owned editions, purchased Music Packs and an active subscription can share a library. One menu does not mean one purchase unlocks everything.

## What belongs to the base song count?

The [35 titles in the launch tracklist](/songs/song-list/) are the count supported by Ubisoft’s announcement. This guide does not add alternate choreographies to that number or include the subscription catalogue. Regional availability and edition-specific extras should be checked against the actual store listing.

| Content source | What the official announcement says |
| --- | --- |
| Decades of Hits | 35 songs in its launch tracklist |
| Owned Just Dance 2023–2026 editions | Their songs are accessible together on the same console |
| Purchased Music Packs | Their purchased songs join that library |
| Active Just Dance+ subscription | Subscription songs can also appear there |

## Do I need to switch games to use earlier purchases?

Ubisoft’s [September announcement](https://news.ubisoft.com/en-us/article/191jAgcwK3nXbzliTZwSOg/just-dance-decades-of-hits-coming-on-october-13) says owners can access their owned 2023–2026 songs in one place **on the same console**, without separate menus or switching games. We retain that console qualification rather than promising all purchases transfer between different platforms or accounts.

## Does Just Dance+ grant the new Adventure Mode?

Nintendo’s [Decades of Hits listing](https://www.nintendo.com/us/store/products/just-dance-decades-of-hits-switch/) labels Adventure Mode as available only with this release. Do not assume a subscription unlocks the mode or that owning a previous edition grants it. The [Adventure guide](/guides/adventure-mode/) explains its separate progression.

## Why does the Nintendo store say I already own the game?

Nintendo’s publisher notification says an “already own” warning may refer to previously downloaded or purchased Just Dance content, including the Free Experience and earlier editions. It does **not** necessarily refer to Decades of Hits. Compare the specific content entitlement in your account before buying; the shared client and the paid song release are distinct.
