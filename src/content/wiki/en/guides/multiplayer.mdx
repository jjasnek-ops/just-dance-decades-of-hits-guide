**Up to six people can dance locally** in Just Dance: Decades of Hits, according to [Ubisoft’s launch article](https://news.ubisoft.com/en-us/article/4C0I0ak0v6u63UZkEdVaEl/just-dance-decades-of-hits-out-now-with-mamma-mia-by-abba-wannabe-by-spice-girls-manchild-by-sabrina-carpenter-and-more). [Version 27.0.1 notes](https://www.ubisoft.com/en-gb/news/ignt.63908/version-27-0-1-patch-notes-article) also confirm local Adventure Mode support for one to six players. Plan controllers for the number of dancers rather than assuming a console purchase equips all six.

## How many people can play together?

| Session | Documented limit | Source scope |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Local dancing | Up to 6 players | Ubisoft launch announcement |
| Adventure Mode local co-op | 1–6 players | Ubisoft 27.0.1 notes |
| Nintendo Switch online | 1–6 players | Nintendo US product listing |

The [Nintendo product page](https://www.nintendo.com/us/store/products/just-dance-decades-of-hits-switch/) lists local and online counts separately. We have not verified every online mode’s cross-platform rules or account invitations on every console, so this page does not claim a universal online setup procedure.

## Can everyone use a phone?

Ubisoft promotes smartphone controller support for local play. Additional accessories may still be needed for your chosen input setup; Nintendo’s launch news includes that qualification. See [controller and offline requirements](/guides/how-to-play/) before mixing phones and Joy-Con controllers.

## Does each Dancer Card have independent progress?

No. The official patch notes say **progression, rewards and scores are shared across all Dancer Cards**. Players can create, rename and customize several cards; local guests can choose existing cards or generated guest profiles during a session. Several profiles do not establish separate save progression.

## What changes when I play online on Switch?

Nintendo’s product page lists Nintendo Switch Online for online play. Ubisoft’s [current help listing](https://www.ubisoft.com/en-us/help/just-dance-2025/all-topics?platform=Nintendo+Switch) names a linked Ubisoft/platform account and an active console online subscription for multiplayer features, and treats Just Dance+ content separately. Those online requirements should not be confused with the six-player local limit. Check current platform-specific subscription wording in the store before paying.
