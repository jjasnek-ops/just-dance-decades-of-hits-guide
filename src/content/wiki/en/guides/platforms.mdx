Just Dance: Decades of Hits is available on **Nintendo Switch, PS5 and Xbox Series X|S** as of October 14, 2026. The dedicated **Switch 2 edition is scheduled for November 10, 2026**. [Ubisoft’s launch announcement](https://news.ubisoft.com/en-us/article/4C0I0ak0v6u63UZkEdVaEl/just-dance-decades-of-hits-out-now-with-mamma-mia-by-abba-wannabe-by-spice-girls-manchild-by-sabrina-carpenter-and-more) and [Nintendo’s launch news](https://www.nintendo.com/us/whatsnew/step-into-a-brand-new-just-dance-experience/) agree on that distinction.

These dates follow official English US launch coverage. Check the linked store for the exact edition and availability in your purchasing region. We have not established a release on PC, PS4 or Xbox One for this title; a series release on an older system does not establish support here.

## Can I play the Switch version on Switch 2 now?

Nintendo’s [US Switch product page](https://www.nintendo.com/us/store/products/just-dance-decades-of-hits-switch/) marks Switch 2 compatibility as supported, with game behavior consistent with Nintendo Switch. That backward-compatible release is distinct from the dedicated Switch 2 version due November 10.

Nintendo says Switch 2 owners can update their Switch game **for free on November 10**, gaining enhanced visuals. An internet connection is required to obtain that update. Do not interpret the future upgrade as already delivered or assume it grants unspecified camera or controller features.
